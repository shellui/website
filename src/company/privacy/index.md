This policy covers the website shellui.com, the presentation site of Shellui, a free and open-source project under the MIT License.

It follows the information duties of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Articles 13 and 14) and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP, Article 19). The Playground at playground.shellui.com has [its own privacy policy](https://playground.shellui.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## 2. What data we process and why

### 2.1 Visiting the site

shellui.com is a static site served by GitHub Pages. When you load a page, GitHub receives your IP address, browser details, the page you asked for and the time, as technical server logs. We do not see or keep these logs.

- Purpose: deliver the site and protect it from abuse.
- Legal basis: legitimate interest in running a working, secure website (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

The site loads no analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no third-party fonts or embeds. Fonts and scripts come from shellui.com itself.

### 2.2 Waitlist and newsletter

When you join the waitlist for the hosted version or subscribe to the newsletter, you give us your email address. We send a confirmation email first. Your address is only added once you click the link in it.

- Data: email address, chosen language, sign-up date, confirmation date.
- Purpose: send you an invite when a spot opens (waitlist), or send you Shellui news (newsletter).
- Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email, or by writing to privacy@shellui.com.

### 2.3 Demo requests

When you request a demo, you give us your email address and, if you want, your name, company, role, team size, what you are building and a message. You confirm your address first, then we reply by email to pick a time.

- Purpose: answer your request and prepare the demo.
- Legal basis: steps you ask for before any agreement (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), and your consent for the optional fields (Art. 6(1)(a)).

### 2.4 Spam and abuse protection

To stop abuse, sign-up forms are rate limited. Your IP address is turned into a one-way code, used only to count requests for about one hour, and is not stored with your sign-up.

- Legal basis: legitimate interest in security (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

### 2.5 Emails you send us

If you write to contact@shellui.com, privacy@shellui.com or another shellui.com address, we receive your address and message through Fastmail. We use them only to answer you.

- Legal basis: legitimate interest in answering you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), or steps you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b)).

We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising and do not profile you.

## 5. How long we keep data

| Data | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Unconfirmed sign-up | Deleted after 7 days |
| Waitlist or newsletter subscription | Until you unsubscribe. Your address, name and form details are then erased after 7 days. A one-way code of your address is kept so we do not add you again by mistake |
| Demo request | Until the demo is done, then 12 months |
| Sent email records in the email service | 30 days [TO CONFIRM: production setting] |

Email addresses are stored encrypted in the email service.

## 6. Your rights

Under the GDPR and the FADP you can:

- access the data we hold about you and get a copy;
- correct inaccurate data;
- have your data deleted;
- restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interest;
- receive your data in a portable format;
- withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

Write to [privacy@shellui.com](mailto:privacy@shellui.com). We answer within 30 days and may ask you to confirm your identity.

## 7. Complaints

You can complain to a supervisory authority:

- Switzerland: Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), Feldeggweg 1, 3003 Bern, [edoeb.admin.ch](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch)
- EU: the authority of the country where you live or work, or where the issue happened. The list is on [edpb.europa.eu](https://www.edpb.europa.eu).

We would like to hear from you first, so we can try to fix it.

## 8. Automated decisions

We make no decisions about you based only on automated processing.

## 9. Cookies and browser storage

shellui.com sets no cookies.

It stores one item in your browser: `theme` in local storage, which remembers whether you picked light or dark mode. It stays until you clear it and never leaves your device. Because it only does what you asked for, it needs no consent.

## 10. Security

The site and forms use HTTPS only. Form addresses are encrypted at rest. Read the [security page](/security/) to report a vulnerability.

If a data breach is likely to put you at risk, we notify the competent authority (EU: within 72 hours, Switzerland: as soon as possible) and tell you when the law requires it.
