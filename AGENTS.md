## Writing

When reviewing or writing prose for [shellui.com](https://shellui.com) or [docs.shellui.com](https://docs.shellui.com), follow the Shellui writing guidelines.

- Skill: [`skills/writing-guidelines/SKILL.md`](skills/writing-guidelines/SKILL.md)
- Canonical rules (versioned): [`content/guidelines/writing.md`](content/guidelines/writing.md)
- Human-readable page: `/guidelines/`

Read the markdown file. Do not scrape the HTML.

Logos and press-kit downloads stay on `/brand-assets/`.
