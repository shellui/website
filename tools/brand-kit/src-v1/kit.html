About this kit

How Shellui looks and reads in print and on screen, so that everything made for Shellui looks like it comes from one place.

Who it is for

Partners, designers, press, and contributors who write about Shellui, design for it, or print for it. Sections 1 to 7 hold the rules, section 8 has print templates to copy, and sections 9 and 10 cover digital assets.

Download the assets

The guidelines live at shellui.com/guidelines/brand, with links to this PDF and the asset zip. The files themselves are served from shellui.com/brand-assets/. The zip mirrors the brand-assets/ folders named in this kit.

og:imageFour link preview images, 1200 x 630
SocialAvatars, X and LinkedIn banners, GitHub preview
EmailHTML email signature

Using the Shellui logo

  • Use the official files, at the sizes and clear space in section 2
  • Do not alter the logo: no redrawing, recoloring, stretching, effects, or lockups with other marks
  • Do not use the logo in a way that suggests Shellui endorses, sponsors, or partners with you unless it does
  • Questions about a use: open an issue at github.com/shellui

Closest matches by CIEDE2000 between the sRGB value and the digital Pantone Solid Coated and Solid Uncoated libraries (swatches.page HEX to Pantone converter, October 2026). Confirm against a physical swatch book and a printed proof.

Under 2 is hard to see; above 5 reads as a different color. Gold ink has no close uncoated spot match: on uncoated stock print it in CMYK and check the proof.

Paper

  • Uncoated, bright white
  • About 120 g/m² for reports and letterhead
  • About 300 g/m² for covers and cards
  • Uncoated paper prints gold duller and type slightly heavier: specify the U references and proof first

Dark pages: when they work

  • Covers, section openers, slides, and one-message pages
  • Showcase pages for dark UI screenshots
  • Not for long reading, forms, or pages people write on
  • One dark page sets a moment; a dark document tires the eye

Dark pages: what to watch

  • Ink: night converts to 296% total ink. Ask your printer for its coverage limit and a rich black build, above all on uncoated stock
  • Contrast: mist on night reads 19.3:1, dark slate 7.9:1. Gold ink on night is 3.7:1 and fails
  • In print, gold on dark is a fill, rule, or the mark in #E3A512, never text
  • Reversed type: at least 9 pt and weight 400; avoid hairline rules under 0.5 pt

Rules for print and screen

  • Sentence case for every heading. Title case only in nav labels

  • No serif anywhere. Plex Sans in print, the system font stack on the website, Inter in generated images

When to use each layout

  • Full width, the default. Body text, tables, charts, and screenshots span all 12 columns, 170 mm. Use it for every text page

7 Imagery

Imagery and charts

Show real data. A chart makes one point, gold marks that point, and white space sets the chart apart from the text.

Charts

  • The title or caption states the finding, not the metric
  • Gray series (#9CA3AF), one gold highlight (#E3A512) with its value labeled in ink
  • Label lines and bars directly. Skip the legend when you can
  • Bars start at zero. No 3D, no pie with more than three slices
  • IBM Plex Sans for all chart text: axis 7 pt slate, labels 8 pt, tabular figures
  • Gridlines in #E5E7EB, 0.2 pt

Space and captions

  • 10 mm of white space above and below every chart, screenshot, and table
  • A caption under each figure: figure number, the finding in one line, and the source. IBM Plex Sans 8 / 12 pt
  • One figure per reading moment. If two figures do not fit with full spacing, move one to the next page
  • Mark sample figures as example data

2 Architecture

How the shell wraps your app

The shell owns navigation, sign-in, settings, and toasts. Your app runs in an iframe and reads that context through @shellui/sdk.

Shared navigation and identity

Each sample team started with the same problem: three apps, three login screens, and three copies of a settings page. Moving those apps into one shell gave them a single sign-in and one navigation bar.

Because identity-service issues the tokens, every service verifies them the same way. In production it signs tokens with an RSA key and publishes a JWKS, so your API checks a signature instead of calling back to the identity server.

What the shell provides to your app

After you call init, your app receives:

  • The signed-in account and a token you can send to your API
  • Theme and locale, so your app matches the shell
  • Toasts, modals, and drawers rendered by the shell, above the iframe

Starting the SDK

Call init once, before the shell sends settings. The call resolves when the shell answers.

Note. JSON Web Key Set (JWKS): the public keys that services use to verify tokens.

import { init } from "@shellui/sdk";

await init({
  clientId: your_client_id_here,
});

The shell around your web app

Shellui is an open-source web app development platform: a microfrontend shell that wraps your application with shared navigation, authentication, storage, and administration.

What ships today

Shell and SDK
Iframe host with shared navigation, theme, toasts, and modals. Your app talks to it through @shellui/sdk.
Identity
identity-service handles sign-in, magic links, OAuth, and sessions.
Administration
Users, roles, and settings at /admin, embedded in the shell.
Storage and Files
storage-service stores uploads. The Files app lets people browse them.
Email
email-service sends transactional mail, newsletters, and broadcasts.
Hosting
hosting-service deploys and serves apps.

Run it your way

Self-host, free
MIT licensed. Docker images for every service on hub.docker.com/u/shellui.
Shellui hosting
Managed hosting for the shell and its services. Details on shellui.com.
Expert help
Architecture, custom work, and support from the maintainer.

v1, October 2026

Brand gold
#E3A512, the gold on shellui.com, for the logo, fills, rules, and chart highlights in every brand file.
Dark gold
#E8B84A for gold text, links, the mark, and fills on dark screens, including the website in dark mode.
Gold ink
#8A6300 for gold text and links on light backgrounds, on screen and in print. It passes WCAG AA at 5.4:1 on white.
Print color and paper
Pantone references for both golds and a paper recommendation, to confirm on a printed proof.
Type
Website: system font stack. Generated screen images (og:images, social, icons, email): Inter. Print: IBM Plex Sans, with Plex Mono for code.
Layout
Full width is the default; a left title column only on covers, openers, and showcase pages.
Gradients
Print, PDFs, og:images, and social images stay flat. A soft gold wash appears on the website only.
Dark pages
Allowed anywhere in print, with light text and gold only as a fill.
Logo
No lockup in v1. The mark and the wordmark are used separately.
Templates
Company details are dashed placeholders; names, items, and amounts are examples.

Contrast ratios use the WCAG 2.2 formula. Values come from the Shellui website tokens.
